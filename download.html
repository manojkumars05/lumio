Download InfiniX

📱 Android

Download APK
  1. Tap Download APK and wait for the download to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
  3. If Android says "Install blocked", tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install again.

If Google Play Protect shows a warning

This is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store. InfiniX is safe to install.

  1. On the "Blocked by Play Protect" or "App scan recommended" screen, tap More details.
  2. Tap Install anyway.
  3. If it asks to scan the app, tap Don't send (or Scan app if you prefer), then confirm Install.
  4. When the install is done, tap Open.

💻 Windows

  1. Open InfiniX in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the Install icon (⊕) at the end of the address bar.
  3. Or open the ⋮ menu, then Cast, save and share, then Install InfiniX.
  4. InfiniX will appear in your Start menu and on your desktop.
