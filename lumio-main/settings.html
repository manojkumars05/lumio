Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

InfiniX ("we", "our", "the app") is a messaging app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. By creating an account you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information: your name, username, email address, date of birth and an InfiniX ID that we generate for you. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never stored by us in readable form.

Messages and media: the messages and photos you send are stored so they can be delivered to the person you are chatting with.

Technical information: basic device and usage data needed to keep the service secure and working.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in.
  • To deliver messages and show your name and username to people who search for you.
  • To show a birthday greeting to you, on your birthday only.
  • To prevent abuse and respond to support requests.

3. What other users can see

Other users can find you by your InfiniX ID or username and can see your name. Your email address and date of birth are private and are not shown to other users.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by our service providers (Google Firebase for authentication and database hosting) only to operate the app.

5. Retention and deletion

Your information is kept while your account is active. Photos and other media in chats may be deleted automatically after a set time. To request deletion of your account or data, contact support from the Help page.

6. Security

We use industry-standard protections, including encrypted connections and access rules that limit each user to their own data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

7. Children

InfiniX is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the support form on the Help page.
